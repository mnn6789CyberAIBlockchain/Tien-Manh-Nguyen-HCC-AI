MyBistro Allergy & Ingredient Reference

Peanut / Tree Nut Concerns:
- Avocado Citrus Salad contains walnuts.
- No peanut-based sauces are used on the regular menu, but cross-contact is possible in prep areas.

Shellfish Concerns:
- Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice Bowl contains shrimp.

Dairy Concerns:
- Truffle Mushroom Pasta contains parmesan and cream.
- Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice Bowl contains butter and aioli.
- Avocado Citrus Salad contains feta cheese.
- Margherita Flatbread contains mozzarella.
- Matcha Tiramisu contains mascarpone.

Gluten Concerns:
- Truffle Mushroom Pasta contains wheat pasta.
- Korean Spicy Chicken Bowl may contain gluten in sauce and coating.
- Margherita Flatbread contains wheat flour.
- Matcha Tiramisu contains sponge cake.

Egg Concerns:
- Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice Bowl contains aioli.
- Matcha Tiramisu contains eggs.

Soy Concerns:
- Korean Spicy Chicken Bowl contains soy-based sauce.

Important Staff Policy:
Employees must never guarantee a dish is 100% allergen-free. They must always mention possible cross-contact and escalate severe allergy concerns to a manager.
