MyBistro AI Security & Incident Response Notes

If MyBistro AI appears to reveal restricted customer or operational information:
1. Disable public-facing access immediately.
2. Notify the floor manager and operations lead.
3. Review recent chat logs for suspicious prompts.
4. Escalate to the system administrator for prompt injection review.
5. Rotate any exposed internal credentials if needed.

Only managers and system administrators are authorized to disable or re-enable AI shield protections in production.
