MyBistro Staff Training Guide

Guest Greeting Standard:
- Greet every guest within 30 seconds.
- Smile and introduce yourself by name.
- Offer water and explain the most popular menu items.

Upselling Guidance:
- Suggest Mango Passion Cooler with spicy dishes.
- Suggest Matcha Tiramisu after pasta or flatbread orders.
- Recommend Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice Bowl for first-time lunch guests.

Allergy Handling:
- Never guess ingredients.
- Always check the allergy guide or ask a manager.
- If a guest mentions a severe allergy, notify the kitchen and manager immediately.

Complaint Recovery SOP:
- Listen fully without interrupting.
- Apologize sincerely.
- Offer to replace the dish or involve a manager.
- Log repeated complaints for shift review.

New Hire Reminder:
- Menu accuracy is more important than speed.
- If unsure, ask a lead or manager.
